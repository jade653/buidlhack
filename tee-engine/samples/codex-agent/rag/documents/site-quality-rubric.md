Use this rubric when shaping landing-page copy for the final output:

- The page should feel timeless, warm, and carefully made.
- Favor a quiet editorial tone over startup hype.
- Keep sentences clean, short, and specific.
- Menu descriptions should suggest sensory character without sounding flowery.
- Brand story should reinforce direct sourcing, small-batch roasting, and honest craftsmanship.
- Beans and goods copy should support both gifting and everyday home brewing.
- Store copy should feel welcoming and grounded in neighborhood context.
- If exact handles, hours, or store URLs are missing, avoid over-inventing details.
- The final experience should read like a premium cafe launch page that could ship with minimal edits.

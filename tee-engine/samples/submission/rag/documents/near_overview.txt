NEAR Protocol Overview
======================

NEAR Protocol is a sharded, proof-of-stake blockchain designed for
high throughput and low transaction costs. The Nightshade sharding
approach splits the network state into parallel shards.

Key facts:
- Founded: 2018
- Mainnet launch: April 2020
- Native token: NEAR
- Smart contract languages: Rust, AssemblyScript
- Consensus: Nightshade (PoS with dynamic sharding)
